Water hygiene record templates

I work in water hygiene, and most of the paperwork problems I see are records that were never kept up. These templates are what I built to make the routine checks quicker to write down: one reading per row, with a clear pass or fail against the targets you set.

They are record-keeping aids. They don't replace a risk assessment, and keeping them doesn't by itself meet any legal duty. What you record, and how often, should come from your own risk assessment.
