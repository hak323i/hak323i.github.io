What a plumber's job sheet should include (boiler service and repair)

A good job sheet does three jobs at once: it tells you what you did, it tells the customer what they're paying for, and it protects you if there's a dispute months later. Here's what to include, from someone who fills them in for a living.

The sections

1. Job details

Job number, date, arrival and departure times, engineer's name and, for gas work, your registration number. Add the customer's name, the site address (if different from billing) and a contact number.

2. What was reported

Write down the customer's words: "no hot water since Tuesday, heating fine". It saves arguments later about what you were asked to fix.

3. Appliance details

Make, model, serial number, location, fuel type and flue type. It's quick to photograph the data plate and copy it later.

4. Readings and checks

Record actual figures, not ticks: working and standing pressures, gas rate, combustion analyser readings, system pressure, and flow and return temperatures. Note visual and flue checks and whether safety devices operated correctly. Compare readings with the manufacturer's instructions for that appliance.

5. Work done and parts used

Plain English, in order. List parts with quantities and part numbers, so warranty claims and invoices match.

6. Advice and anything left unsafe

If you find a dangerous appliance, follow the industry unsafe situations procedure and record exactly what you did and told the customer. HSE's domestic gas safety pages set out duties under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998, including that gas work must be done by a registered engineer.

7. Sign-off

Customer's signature and printed name, your signature, and whether payment was taken or an invoice will follow.

Tips for job sheets that work

  • Fill them in on site, not in the van at 7pm.
  • Take before and after photos and note the photo numbers on the sheet.
  • Give the customer a copy the same day; it reads as professional and cuts down "what did you actually do?" calls.
  • Keep a separate service record for landlords. Your gas safety record is a different document with its own requirements (see HSE).
