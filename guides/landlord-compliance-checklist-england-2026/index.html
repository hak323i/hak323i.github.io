Landlord compliance checklist for England 2026: certificates and dates to track

Most compliance problems aren't about not knowing the rules. They're about a renewal date slipping past. Here are the main items for a typical private let in England, how often each comes round, and a worked example of the dates.

Checked against GOV.UK, HSE and legislation.gov.uk on 10 October 2026. This applies to England; Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have different rules. Licensing (HMO, selective) varies by council, so check with yours.

Renters' Rights Act: what changed on 1 May 2026

  • Assured shorthold tenancies became assured periodic tenancies, and section 21 "no-fault" notices can no longer be used. Possession needs a section 8 ground. See GOV.UK: repossessing your property after 1 May 2026.
  • Rent increases go through the section 13 (Form 4A) process, with at least two months' notice and no more than once a year.
  • No rent before the tenancy is signed, and no more than one month's rent between signing and the start date.
  • You must publish an asking rent and can't accept offers above it.
  • A tenant can ask in writing to keep a pet. You must reply in writing within 28 days (extendable by 7 days if you ask the tenant for more information) and can't refuse unreasonably.
  • Tenants must be given written information about key terms. For existing written tenancies, the government's information sheet was due by 31 May 2026. It must be the official GOV.UK PDF, given to every named tenant, and fines go up to £7,000.

Some proposed changes, including the private rented sector database and extending Awaab's Law to private lets, were not in force when this was checked. The new private rented sector landlord ombudsman isn't in force yet either. Watch GOV.UK for dates rather than acting on headlines.

Worked example: working out the next due dates

ItemLast doneNext due by
Gas safety check14/03/202613/03/2027

On gas: HSE allows the next check to be done up to two months before the deadline while keeping the original expiry date. Here, a check any time from 14/01/2027 keeps the anniversary at March, as long as you keep the records that show it.

All three certificates fall due in the same six months of 2027. That's why a single tracker with early warnings beats a pile of PDFs in your email.

Tips

  • Set reminders two months before each date, not on the day.
  • Keep proof that you served documents (an email with the attachment is fine), not just the documents themselves.
  • Re-check this list after any change of tenant, appliance or legislation.
